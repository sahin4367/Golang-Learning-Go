|--Yeni dilimler , bu ARRAYI , yeni massivi dilimlere bolur!
A slice keeps three things : slice 3 seyi saxlayir
1. Pointers - A reference to the underlying array.
2. Lenghts - The number of elements in the slice.
3. Capasity - The maximum number of elements the slice can hold
